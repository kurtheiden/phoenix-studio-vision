> original studio vision profiles. Anything that says MIDI was generated by

"Profiles" is Steve's wording. No specific technical meaning is assigned to
it, and it is not equated with Phoenix's technical use of "profile."

## Evidence-bounded conclusions

1. Steve identifies the non-MIDI files as the original files/source material he
   used in Studio Vision and from which the later ChatGPT-generated MIDI
   derivatives were made.
2. Steve identifies the files designated or labeled as MIDI as later
   derivatives generated by submitting the original material to ChatGPT.
3. Steve reports that those ChatGPT conversions "didn't do a great job."
4. Those ChatGPT-generated MIDI derivatives must not be treated as authoritative
   Studio Vision reference exports or ground truth for Phoenix validation.
5. The derivatives must remain unread and uninspected during the upcoming
   blind-validation phase.
6. Their existence does not disqualify that original source material
   from independent Phoenix blind validation, provided the originals otherwise
   meet the adopted eligibility requirements. This statement does not establish
   eligibility or select a candidate.
7. The derivatives may potentially be examined much later as historical
   third-party conversion artifacts, only after the relevant Phoenix blind
   observation/reference work has been frozen and only if useful. No such
   examination is part of this task or authorized by this document.
